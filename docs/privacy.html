SoulCap Privacy

Effective 14 September 2026 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

Summary

SoulCap works on your device. We don’t have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and we don’t sell data.

What stays on your device

  • Profile name and optional details
  • Check-ins, journal entries, and related notes
  • People map (Constellation) and optional links
  • Technique history, favourites, and feedback
  • Principles, personal manual, reflection and screener results
  • Appearance, language, and accessibility preferences
  • Optional app-lock metadata (passcode wrap only — your passcode is never stored in plain text)

What leaves your device

ServiceWhat is sentWhyWhen
Nothing. The shipped PWA makes no third-party network requests. Optional on-device speech (when the browser supports it) never leaves the device.

Your controls

Export your data: Settings → Privacy & Data → Export everything.

Erase all data: Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete everything, permanently.

App lock: Settings → Privacy & Data → App lock (optional).

Children

SoulCap is for adults 18 and over.
